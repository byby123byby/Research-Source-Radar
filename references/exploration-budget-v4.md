# Exploration Budget v4

This reference explains how to preserve less-obvious candidates within the current unified output contract. Numeric limits live only in `runtime-contract-v1.json`.

## Candidate Schedule

1. Search exact/decomposed terms, a mechanism neighbor, and an artifact or current-work ecosystem.
2. Normalize identities and duplicates immediately; retain compact metadata only.
3. Probe the highest-value empty family, such as validation, failure, or a concrete adjacent bridge.
4. Open only candidates whose identity or evidence could change the ranking.
5. Stop at the declared budget and report unsearched or timed-out paths.

## Selection

Require a canonical or explicitly unresolved identity, two target anchors, and a concrete bridge before ranking. Use one ranked list with `direct`, `mechanism`, `validation`, `current`, and `adjacent` roles. Research impact and deployment fit remain separate: an incompatible implementation can still contribute a valuable mechanism, but its limitation must be explicit.

Popularity, citations, repeated indexing, and model confidence cannot repair a failed relevance or identity gate. A broader pool is useful only when it improves blinded recovery of valuable sources without reducing ranking quality or completion.

## Context Boundary

Keep only canonical identity, source type, route, one relevance sentence, one mechanism bridge, one fit label, and one unresolved risk in the working ledger. Do not carry raw result pages, full abstracts, README bodies, or duplicate metadata across search batches.
